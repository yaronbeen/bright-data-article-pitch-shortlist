# Article Pitch Shortlist

Check where your article belongs before spending time on a pitch.

Your agent collects your article or outline and selected publisher examples and guidelines through [Bright Data](https://brightdata.com). It checks topic, format, and submission-route fit, then drafts one short pitch only when the sources support a match. Nothing is sent.

## What You Get

- A small shortlist separating supported fits, holds, and exclusions.
- Source-backed fit reasons and missing requirements to check.
- One personalized pitch draft for human review, without an acceptance promise.

## Give This To Your Agent

```text
Use pitch-fit-personalizer for [my public article/outline URL] and [up to
three publishers' example and guideline URLs]. My proposed contribution
is [topic, audience, format, and what the piece adds]; publication/reuse
status is [status]. Collect the source pages through my configured Bright
Data tools. Check topic, format, and submission-route fit. Return fits,
holds, and exclusions with quotes, source URLs, capture times, and one
short pitch only for a supported fit. If Bright Data is not connected,
ask me to connect it and stop. Do not enrich contacts, send, or submit.
```

Read the [pitch-fit-personalizer skill](skills/pitch-fit-personalizer/SKILL.md).
Connect your agent using the [official Bright Data MCP setup](https://docs.brightdata.com/products/mcp-server/remote/quickstart) or this [short connection guide](docs/technical-guide.md).
