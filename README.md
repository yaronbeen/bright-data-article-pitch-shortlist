# Article Pitch Shortlist

Find publishers whose examples and guidelines support a pitch for your article.

Your agent uses [Bright Data](https://brightdata.com) to collect your article and selected publisher pages. The bundled skill checks those sources before drafting.

## What You Get

- A shortlist separating supported matches, holds and exclusions.
- Source-backed fit reasons and a checklist of missing submission requirements.
- One short personalized pitch draft for human review, only when the evidence supports it.

## Give This To Your Agent

```text
Use [my article/outline URL] and [article facts, audience, proposed
topic/title, contribution and format]. My proposal details are [follow-up
or reuse method, publication/originality status, planned length and
worked-example availability]. With my configured Bright Data search and
page-collection tools, collect the article and find publishers covering
[topic]. Ask me to select up to five publishers, then collect at most one
article example and one guidelines page per publisher. Follow
pitch-fit-personalizer: return matched, held and excluded cards, plus one
short draft only for a supported fit. Cite observed sources, dates and
unknowns. Ask for missing inputs or Bright Data exports if tools are
unavailable. Do not contact anyone or submit.
```

Skill: [pitch-fit-personalizer](skills/pitch-fit-personalizer/SKILL.md).

In the [checked example (invented data)](docs/skills/pitch-fit-personalizer-example.md), North gets a draft, West is excluded because submissions are closed, and East stays on hold.
